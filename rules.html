General Rules

1. Maintain Silence

Keep the library quiet and respect other readers.

2. Return Books on Time

Return borrowed books on or before the due date.

3. Handle Books Carefully

Do not tear pages, write in books or damage covers.

4. Follow Borrowing Limits

Follow the borrowing limit and loan period set by the library.

5. Keep the Library Clean

Do not bring food or drinks near library books and computers.

6. Late Returns

Late-return fines, if applicable, follow the library's official policy.

7. Respect Library Property

Take care of furniture, computers and other library resources.
